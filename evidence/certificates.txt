HIERARCHY (Corda dev-mode PKI, same structure as a production Corda Network):

  Corda Root CA  (self-signed, in truststore.jks of every node)
    +-- Corda Doorman / Intermediate CA  (issues node CA certificates)
    |     +-- Node CA  'O=EnterpriseX,...'  (cordaclientca in nodekeystore.jks)
    |           +-- Legal identity  (identity-private-key, Ed25519)  -> signs transactions
    |           +-- TLS certificate (cordaclienttls in sslkeystore.jks, ECDSA P-256) -> mutual TLS for P2P/AMQP
    +-- Network Map CA  (signs the network map / network parameters; not stored in the node keystores)
